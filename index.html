Featured product

Jaap Counter

Jaap Counter is a Hindu devotional mantra-counting Android app—a digital mala—for tracking japs, goals, streaks, practice time, and meditation.

  • Tap, automatic, and volume-button counting modes.
  • Multiple mantra profiles, journey summaries, themes, widgets, and local backup.
  • Optional private Google Drive backup for users who choose cloud snapshots.
