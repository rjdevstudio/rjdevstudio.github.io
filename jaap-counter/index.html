Features

Designed around devotional counting

Flexible counting

Use tap counting, Automatic mode, or volume-button counting depending on your practice setting.

Profiles and progress

Create multiple mantra profiles with daily goals, streaks, lifetime totals, journey history, and practice-time tracking.

Meditation support

Use the meditation timer, home-screen widget, themes, optional profile wallpapers, and English or Hindi interface.

Backup options

  • Export and restore local backup files.
  • Use optional private Google Drive backup stored in Drive's hidden appDataFolder.
  • The newest two complete Drive snapshots are retained.
  • Wallpaper files can be preserved in Drive snapshots when Drive backup is used.
  • Jaap Counter has no ads.

Premium

Premium is a one-time Google Play unlock for the supported app. It enables Drive backup creation and automatic backup.

Restoring an existing Drive backup remains available without Premium. Google Play controls the displayed regional price and manages purchase and refund processes.

Google Play Billing does not provide the purchaser's Google email to the app.
