Data Management

Manage or delete Jaap Counter data

These instructions help you manage local app data, optional hidden Google Drive backup data, and privacy questions about diagnostics.

Local data

  1. Export a local backup first if you want to keep a copy before deletion.
  2. Use in-app Reset options where applicable.
  3. Clear Android application storage from your device settings if you want to remove the app's local storage.
  4. Uninstall Jaap Counter to remove app data managed by Android on that device.

Google Drive data

Disconnecting Drive stops future access and automatic backup. Existing hidden backups may remain in Google Drive until you delete hidden app data.

Delete hidden app data

  1. Open Google Drive on the web.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Open Manage apps.
  4. Find Jaap Counter.
  5. Choose the option to delete hidden app data, if available.

Revoke Google access

You can also open your Google Account's third-party connections or security settings, find Jaap Counter, and revoke access. Revoking access stops future authorization but does not necessarily remove existing hidden backup data.

Firebase diagnostic data

Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics records are generally pseudonymous technical records and may not be directly searchable by an email address. For privacy questions, contact rjdevstudio@gmail.com.
