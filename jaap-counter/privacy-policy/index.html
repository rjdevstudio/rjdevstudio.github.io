Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how RJ Dev Studio handles information for Jaap Counter. RJ Dev Studio is operated from India. Contact us at rjdevstudio@gmail.com.

Local app data

Jaap Counter stores primary practice data locally on your device. This includes profiles and mantra names; counts, goals, history, streak-related data, and practice time; preferences and settings; meditation timer settings; wallpaper metadata and wallpaper image files; and local backup files created by you.

There is no RJ Dev Studio account system and no RJ Dev Studio backend storing this practice data.

Optional Google Drive backup

Google Drive backup is optional and user initiated. Connecting a Google account alone does not upload data. When you choose Drive backup, Jaap Counter requests only OpenID, Google account email/profile information, and the drive.appdata permission.

Backup files are stored in Google Drive's hidden appDataFolder. They do not appear as normal files in My Drive. Jaap Counter cannot browse unrelated Drive files. Snapshots can contain profiles, counts, history, settings, practice time, and wallpaper bytes.

The newest two complete Drive snapshots are retained. Old snapshots and unreferenced wallpaper blobs are automatically cleaned up. Google OAuth access tokens remain in memory and are not stored as refresh tokens. No RJ Dev Studio server receives the backup contents.

The connected Google email may be stored locally on the device so the app can show which Drive account is connected. Disconnecting stops automatic backup. Disconnecting or revoking access does not necessarily delete existing hidden Drive backup data.

Firebase services

Jaap Counter uses Firebase Analytics for aggregated product-usage information and Firebase Crashlytics for crash and diagnostic information. Information processed by Google/Firebase may include app version, device and operating-system information, app interactions, crash traces, diagnostic and performance-related metadata, and technical identifiers handled by Firebase.

This information is used to maintain reliability and improve the product. Learn more from Google's privacy information.

Google Play Billing

Premium purchases and voluntary offerings are processed by Google Play. RJ Dev Studio does not directly receive payment-card information. Google Play Billing does not provide the purchaser's Google email to the app. Purchases are governed by Google Play's terms and refund processes.

Notifications and device features

Jaap Counter may use local notifications for reminders and summaries, Android foreground-service notifications for explicitly enabled background automatic counting, the Android System Photo Picker for wallpaper selection, haptics, and volume-button input. Selected wallpaper images are stored locally and may be included in Drive snapshots if Drive backup is used.

Data sharing

RJ Dev Studio does not sell personal data. Jaap Counter has no advertisements. Data is shared with service providers only as required for optional Drive functionality, Google Play Billing, Firebase Analytics, and Crashlytics.

Retention

Local data remains until you reset the app, clear application storage, or uninstall the app. Drive keeps the newest two snapshots until they are replaced or you delete hidden app data. Firebase data follows the configured Firebase/Google retention policies.

User choices

  • Use local backup without Google Drive.
  • Disable automatic Drive backup or disconnect Drive.
  • Revoke Google access from your Google Account security settings.
  • Delete hidden Drive application data from Google Drive settings.
  • Reset local app data, clear Android application storage, or uninstall the app.
  • Contact rjdevstudio@gmail.com for privacy questions.

Security

RJ Dev Studio uses platform security features and limited permissions to protect app data. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so please keep your device, Google account, and backups protected.

Children's privacy

Jaap Counter is a general-audience devotional and productivity app. It is not directed to children, and RJ Dev Studio does not knowingly collect children's personal information through an RJ Dev Studio account system.

International processing

Service providers such as Google may process technical, billing, analytics, diagnostic, or Drive-related information in locations outside your country according to their own infrastructure and policies.

Changes and contact

RJ Dev Studio may update this policy as the app or legal requirements change. Updated versions will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Contact: rjdevstudio@gmail.com.
