Frequently asked questions

How do I back up locally?

Use Jaap Counter's local backup/export option where available, choose a safe location you control, and keep a copy before resetting, clearing storage, or changing devices.

How does Drive backup work?

Drive backup is optional. When enabled, Jaap Counter creates private snapshots in Google Drive's hidden appDataFolder using the limited Drive app data permission.

Why are Drive backups hidden?

The appDataFolder is designed for application data. Backups do not appear as normal My Drive files, and the app cannot browse unrelated Drive files.

Can I restore without Premium?

Yes. Restoring an existing Drive backup remains available without Premium. Premium enables Drive backup creation and automatic backup.

How do I switch Google accounts?

Disconnect Drive in the app, then connect again with the Google account you want to use. Existing hidden backups under another account may remain in that account until deleted from Drive settings.

What happens when I disconnect Drive?

Disconnecting stops future Drive access and automatic backup. It does not necessarily delete existing hidden backup data already stored in Google Drive.

How do I delete hidden Drive data?

See the Data Management page for steps to delete hidden app data from Google Drive settings.

How do I restore Premium?

Use the restore purchase option in Jaap Counter where available and make sure Google Play is signed into the account that purchased Premium.

Are wallpapers included in backups?

Selected wallpaper images are stored locally and may be included in Drive snapshots when Drive backup is used.

When does automatic backup run?

Automatic backup timing can vary. Android may defer background work based on battery, connectivity, and system conditions.

Does the app work offline?

Core counting and local data features are designed for offline use. Google Drive backup, Google Play purchase checks, and Firebase services require connectivity when they run.

Why are notification permissions needed?

Notifications may be used for reminders, summaries, and foreground-service notifications for explicitly enabled background automatic counting.

How does volume-button mode work?

When volume-button counting is enabled, the app can count using hardware volume button input while the feature is active.

What should I include in a bug report?

Please include app version, Android version, device model, steps to reproduce the issue, and a screenshot if appropriate.
